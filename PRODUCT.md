# Product Context

## Overview

MazyLabs is a Campinas-based technology consultancy that designs and implements data, artificial intelligence, automation, integration, and custom software solutions for small and medium-sized businesses. The website exists to generate qualified local leads and demonstrate that the company delivers production systems with engineering rigor rather than AI hype.

## Target Users

- Owners and operations leaders at small and medium-sized businesses in Campinas and the surrounding region.
- Technology and data leaders who need integrations, pipelines, dashboards, AI agents, document processing, or custom systems.
- Teams with manual, fragmented, or unreliable processes that need a practical diagnostic, pilot, implementation, and knowledge transfer.

## Brand Personality

Direct, rigorous, practical, transparent, and technically credible. The company should feel approachable to business decision-makers without diluting its engineering depth.

## Voice & Tone

- Explain outcomes in plain Portuguese and support claims with concrete delivery methods.
- Prefer specific language about data, AI, automation, APIs, documents, dashboards, and systems.
- Avoid inflated promises, generic innovation language, and unsupported claims about guaranteed results.
- Invite a low-friction conversation through WhatsApp or email.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the current terracotta, stone, bone, and dark visual identity.
- Use strong editorial hierarchy, restrained motion, and clear calls to action.
- Local relevance should be explicit but natural, never keyword-stuffed.
- Default to WCAG AA contrast, keyboard usability, semantic HTML, and reduced-motion support.
- Mobile visitors must be able to understand the offer and contact MazyLabs quickly.

## Anti-References

- Generic AI-startup aesthetics, neon gradients, and vague futuristic imagery.
- Template SaaS copy and claims such as “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” or guaranteed growth.
- Fake offices, invented customer results, fabricated testimonials, or misleading local addresses.
